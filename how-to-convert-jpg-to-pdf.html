How to Convert Multiple JPG Images to a Single PDF – Step by Step

Published: January 15, 2025 | By onPDF Team

Converting multiple JPG images into a single PDF document is a common need for professionals, students, and everyday users. Whether you want to combine scanned documents, create a portfolio, or simply organize several images into one file, our free online tool makes the process quick and effortless. In this guide, we'll walk you through every step.

Why Combine JPGs into One PDF?

PDF files are universally compatible, preserve formatting, and are easier to share than multiple image files. A single PDF reduces clutter and ensures that all pages stay in the correct order. It is particularly useful for:

  • Scanning multi-page documents
  • Submitting assignments or applications
  • Creating e-books or manuals
  • Archiving receipts or invoices

Step 1: Prepare Your Images

Before you start, make sure all the JPG (or PNG) images you want to include are saved on your device. You can arrange them in a folder and rename them in the order you want them to appear in the PDF. While our tool allows you to reorder images after uploading, having them pre-sorted saves time.

Step 2: Upload Your Images

Go to the onPDF homepage. You can either click the upload area to browse your files or simply drag and drop them into the designated zone. The tool accepts JPG, JPEG, and PNG formats. You can add up to 50 images at once, each up to 25MB.

Step 3: Arrange and Preview

After uploading, you'll see thumbnails of all your images in a grid. Each thumbnail has arrows to move the image left or right, and an 'X' to remove it. Use these controls to arrange the images in the exact order you want them to appear in the PDF. You can also drag and drop the thumbnails to reorder them.

Step 4: Choose Settings

Before converting, select your preferred page size (A4, Letter, Legal, A5, or A3) and orientation (Portrait, Landscape, or Auto). The "Auto" option intelligently fits each image based on its own dimensions, which is great for mixed content. For most standard documents, A4 portrait works well.

Step 5: Convert and Download

Click the "Convert to PDF" button. A progress animation will show the conversion status. Within seconds, the tool will generate the PDF and automatically download it to your device. You can also click "Download Again" if needed.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use high-resolution images (at least 150 DPI) for clear text.
  • Ensure all images are properly rotated before uploading.
  • If your PDF is too large, consider compressing images first.

That's it! You now have a single, professional PDF file combining all your JPG images. No sign-up, no hidden costs, and your files never leave your device.
