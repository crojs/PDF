A4 vs Letter vs Legal: Which PDF Page Size Should You Choose?

Published: January 10, 2025 | By onPDF Team

When creating a PDF from JPG images, choosing the right page size matters more than you might think. The wrong size can cause unnecessary margins, cut-off content, or a document that doesn't match regional printing standards. In this article, we compare the three most common page sizes—A4, Letter, and Legal—to help you make an informed decision.

A4 (210 × 297 mm)

A4 is the international standard used in most countries around the world, including Europe, Asia, and Australia. It's slightly narrower and taller than the North American Letter size. A4 is ideal for business documents, academic papers, and general correspondence. If you're sharing your PDF globally, A4 is a safe choice.

Letter (216 × 279 mm)

Letter is the standard size in the United States, Canada, and parts of Latin America. It is slightly wider and shorter than A4. If your audience is primarily in North America, Letter is the preferred choice. Many American companies and universities expect documents in Letter format.

Legal (216 × 356 mm)

Legal size is longer than Letter, providing extra vertical space. It is commonly used for contracts, legal documents, and real estate paperwork. However, it is less common for everyday use and may not be ideal for image-based PDFs unless the images are tall (like screenshots of long webpages).

Other Sizes: A5 and A3

Our tool also offers A5 (half of A4) and A3 (twice A4). A5 is handy for booklets or small notes, while A3 is suitable for posters and large diagrams. These are less common but can be useful in specific scenarios.

How to Choose the Right Size

  • Consider your audience: If you're unsure where your PDF will be printed or viewed, A4 is the most versatile.
  • Check the source images: If your images are standard photo sizes (4×6, etc.), a smaller page like A5 might save space.
  • Use Auto orientation: Our tool's Auto mode automatically selects portrait or landscape based on each image, which can help you avoid awkward white space.

Remember, you can always try different page sizes and download the result instantly. Since conversion is free and local, you can experiment without any cost. For most users, A4 or Letter will work perfectly.
