Frequently Asked Questions

Is this JPG to PDF converter free to use?

Yes, our JPG to PDF converter is completely free. There are no hidden charges or sign-up requirements. All conversions happen directly in your browser, ensuring your files remain private.

How many images can I convert at once?

You can upload up to 50 images at a time. Each image can be up to 25MB in size. The tool supports JPG, JPEG, and PNG formats.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Your images are processed locally on your device using JavaScript. They are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Can I change the page size and orientation?

Yes, you can choose from A4, Letter, Legal, A5, and A3 page sizes. You can also set orientation to Portrait, Landscape, or Auto, which intelligently fits each image.

Do I need to install any software?

No, this is a completely online tool. It works in any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile devices.
