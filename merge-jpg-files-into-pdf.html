How to Merge Multiple JPG Files into One PDF Document

Published: December 28, 2024 | By onPDF Team

Merging multiple JPG images into a single PDF is a straightforward process with the right tool. Whether you're combining scanned pages, photos, or screenshots, this guide will show you how to do it efficiently using onPDF's free converter.

Why Merge JPGs into a PDF?

Merging images into one PDF makes it easier to share and store. Instead of sending a folder full of images, you send one file. It also ensures that the order of pages is preserved, which is crucial for documents like contracts or reports.

Preparing Your Files

  • Collect all JPG images you want to merge.
  • Rename them in sequential order (e.g., page1.jpg, page2.jpg) to simplify sorting.
  • Check that each image is properly rotated and cropped.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Visit the onPDF JPG to PDF tool.
  2. Click the upload area or drag and drop your images. You can select multiple files at once.
  3. Once uploaded, you'll see thumbnails. Use the arrows on each thumbnail to rearrange them.
  4. Choose your desired page size and orientation.
  5. Click "Convert to PDF". The tool will process the images and create a single PDF.
  6. Download the PDF automatically.

Advanced Tips

If you have images with mixed orientations, select "Auto" in the orientation setting. This will automatically set each page to portrait or landscape based on the image's aspect ratio, resulting in a cleaner output.

For images with different aspect ratios, adding a margin (by reducing the scale slightly) can help avoid unwanted cropping. Our tool automatically centers and fits images within the page, maintaining aspect ratio.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Uploading images in the wrong order: Always double-check the thumbnail order before converting.
  • Choosing an incompatible page size: If your images are extremely wide, Letter or A4 portrait may leave large blank areas. Consider using landscape orientation.
  • Overlooking file size limits: Each image must be under 25MB, so compress very large files beforehand.
